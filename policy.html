Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

This privacy policy describes how Run Brian (sirbrian88.github.io/runbrian) handles information when you visit this site.

Information We Collect

This site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous data about site visits including pages viewed, approximate location (country/city level), device type, and referral source. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

If you leave a comment using the Disqus comment system, Disqus collects your name, email address, and comment content. Disqus has its own privacy policy available at disqus.com.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Disqus set cookies in your browser to track sessions and preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Google Analytics data collection can be opted out of via Google's opt-out tool.

Data Sharing

This site does not sell or share your personal data with third parties beyond the analytics and commenting services described above.
